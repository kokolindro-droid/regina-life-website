## 2. How these were selected (method)

I searched the open web for longevity / anti-aging / preventive / functional-medicine clinics across the Balkan region, then filtered for sites that (a) are genuinely medical (named physicians, biomarkers, diagnostics), (b) put **longevity** at the centre of the message, (c) have a real **team** section, and (d) use **motion** meaningfully. Each shortlisted site was retrieved as text and rendered as an image so the design, structure and motion cues could be verified rather than guessed.

**Regional sweep performed:** Bulgaria, Serbia, Slovenia, Croatia, Greece, Romania. Romania returned only agencies (e.g. Prowebdesign), not clinic brands, so it was dropped; Croatia's clinic sites (Poliklinika Preventiva, Ordinacija Ostojic) were weaker on longevity positioning.

## 3. The five reference websites

### 1) Blue Longevity Clinic — Bulgaria 🇧🇬
**URL:** https://blue-longevity.bg (English: https://blue-longevity.bg/en) · **City:** Sofia, 51D Cherni Vrah Blvd, Lozenets · **Languages:** Bulgarian + English + (network) Greek/Turkish context

**What it is.** A dedicated longevity clinic that says it is "the first longevity clinic in Bulgaria," part of an international network with locations in **Sofia · Athens · Istanbul**.[1] It raised **€2M** to roll out longevity centres across Southeast Europe.[13][14]

**Positioning line (translated):** *"The first longevity clinic in Bulgaria — evidence-based longevity, 80+ biomarkers and a personal protocol with a doctor."*[1]
Mission on-site: *"Adding healthy years to life through lifestyle, science and technology."*[1]

**Longevity angle.** 80+ lab biomarkers, **TruAge** biological age of 11 body systems, functional tests (VO₂ max, RMR, HRV, grip strength, body composition/phase angle, sleep-apnoea test), five DNA panels (Hormones, Health & Nutrition, Skin, Sport, APOE), gut-microbiome molecular analysis, and longevity therapies — **HBOT, cryotherapy, IHHT, red-light therapy**.[1]

**Medical credibility signals.** Physician-led protocols; press wall featuring **Capital, Forbes Bulgaria, Bloomberg TV, Nova, bTV, Investor.bg, SeeNews, Health Tech World**; a doctor-consultation CTA in the header; a "Longevity FAQ" and "science of longevity" content hub.[1]

**Team section (what to copy).** A full **management + medical team** page with photos, roles and expandable bios:
- **Ilian Grigorov** — Founder & CEO (built City Clinic / Acibadem City Clinic; MHA Minnesota; MSc USC; Harvard) 
- **Blagoy Palev** — CFO & Co-founder · **Veronika Nakova** — General Manager, Sofia
- **Dr. Vesselin Dimitrov Gavrilov** — Chief Medical Officer, Sofia (anaesthesiology/ICU, 15+ yrs)
- **Dr. Viktoria Dimitrova** — Functional Cardiologist · **RN Metodinka Borisova** — Head Nurse
- **Nikolay Polendakov** — CIO · **Jasmina Gevezieva** — Marketing Director
- **Atanas Raykov** — Strategic AI & Digital Transformation Advisor (ex-Rakuten Viber)[2]

**Animation / motion observed.** Full-bleed **hero video**; horizontal **therapy and program carousels** (prev/next controls); an interactive **"Longevity Score" quiz** (10 questions with pillar labels); a scrolling **press-logo marquee**; step-counters (01–04).[1]

**Best takeaway for a Bulgarian site.** This is your closest competitor *and* your closest template. Copy: the **"first in Bulgaria" claim** (if defensible), the **80+ biomarkers** proof number, the **header "Doctor consultation" CTA**, the **expert/advisor row** (it adds authority beyond clinicians), and the **free assessment quiz** as a lead magnet.

### 2) Epik — Slovenia 🇸🇮
**URL:** https://epik.care (English: https://epik.care/en/) · **City:** Ljubljana (Kranjčeva ulica 34), originally Kranj · **Languages:** Slovenian + English

**What it is.** "Slovenia's first longevity clinic," built around predictive & preventive medicine and a year-long guided programme.[3]

**Positioning line (translated):** *"Health that we measure, explain and guide."* and *"The question is not how many years you will live. The question is what you will be like in them."*[3]

**Longevity angle.** "Longevity Plan" as a deliverable document with a **longevity index** and a timeline of follow-ups; **60+ lab tests** (hormones, vitamins, metabolic, inflammation, cardiovascular), **DEXA** body composition, **VO₂ max** stress testing, **continuous glucose monitoring (CGM)**, neck-vein and abdominal ultrasound, plus an add-on **multi-cancer early-detection blood test (>70 cancers)**.[3]

**Team section (what to copy).** A **team carousel** ("The team of experts who guide your process") with portraits, names and clinical titles:
**Marija Lucija Antolič** (occupational, traffic & sports medicine) · **Lucijan Kobal** (radiology) · **Maša Marija Vrtačnik** (MD) · **Tina Trtnik** (nutrition adviser) · **Eva Natlačen** (mental-resilience adviser).[3][4]

**Animation / motion observed.** **Video hero**; a **01–04 circular process diagram** (Diagnostics → Clinical analysis → Longevity Plan → Action) that draws in on scroll; **animated progress bars** in the programme comparison; **segmented programme tabs** (Starter / One / Premium / Full); accordions for "Experience" and FAQ (with rotating +/− icons); team carousel; and an **"Ask AI about Epik"** assistant widget.[3]

**Best takeaway for a Bulgarian site.** Steal the **derived deliverable** idea — sell a *named document* ("План за дълголетие" / "Longevity Plan" with a longevity index and a 12-month timeline), not just tests. Also steal the **4-step ring diagram** and **programme comparison tabs**, which explain a complex, expensive product simply. Accreditations: it explicitly differentiates itself from a standard "manager's health check".

### 3) Notia Medcare — Greece 🇬🇷
**URL:** https://www.notiamedcare.eu · **City:** Athens (Glyfada), 127 Vouliagmenis Ave, ~3,000 m² · **Languages:** English + Greek

**What it is.** A large, well-funded multidisciplinary **Health & Longevity Center** positioned as "the first and only multidisciplinary, evidence-based Medical Longevity Center in Greece," with a longevity centre, primary-care unit, aesthetic/dermatology unit and a forthcoming one-day-surgery clinic.[7][8]

**Positioning line:** *"Add Life To Years — Pioneering a New Era in Longevity and Multidisciplinary Medicine."*[7]

**Longevity angle.** Exhaustive panels (genetics/epigenetics, microbiome, cardiometabolic, neurocognitive, hormones, VO₂ max, resting metabolic rate); protocols named for outcomes (NeuroLongevity, Metabolic Health, Cardio-Metabolic, Peak Performance, Bio-age, CEOs & Executives); interventions include **HBOT, IV drips, PEMF, contrast therapy, plasma purification**; an on-site lab and a **patient portal** for records and booking.[8]

**Medical credibility signals.** **16 departments** with a scientific director per department; "more than 15 specialists" / "more than 20 physicians"; AI-assisted diagnostics and continuous biomarker monitoring. Pricing is premium: **from ~$3,000, average ~$8,000 per client**.[7][8]

**Team section — ⚠️ important gap.** Despite claiming 15–20+ specialists, the site **names no physician anywhere** and had **no reachable team page** when independently checked.[9] For a Bulgarian build, this is the *anti-pattern*: if you promise a multidisciplinary team, **name it** (see Blue Longevity / Epik).

**Animation / motion observed (strongest of the five).** A **letter-by-letter animated headline** ("A d d  L i f e  T o  Y e a r s"); a **full-bleed hero slider**; a **"persona" slider** (John / Anna / Paul, 1-of-3) mapping goals to treatments; **video-backed service cards** for each unit; a 7-item **service carousel**; journey steps 1–2–3 with imagery.[7]

**Best takeaway for a Bulgarian site.** Use Notia as your **motion reference** (how to animate a hero and persona-select panels) — but use Blue Longevity/Epik for content structure. Note also the **legal/reputational notice** pinned to the top of the Notia homepage (the clinic addressing media reports and a judicial-investigation association).[7] For a medical brand, always keep a truthful, verifiable paper trail; a competitor's crisis is a reminder not to over-claim.

### 4) Rado Health — Serbia 🇷🇸
**URL:** https://www.radohealth.com · **City:** Belgrade, Tadeuša Košćuška 63 · **Languages:** Serbian (no English version found)

**What it is.** "The first Center for Longevity and Functional Medicine in Serbia," combining functional medicine with advanced diagnostics, hormone optimisation, supplementation, IV therapy, longevity aesthetics and lifestyle medicine.[5][24]

**Positioning line (translated):** *"Optimise hormones, energy, stress, metabolism and recovery."* and *"This is where science and personalisation meet… the goal is for the years ahead to be quality years, not just more numerous."*[5]

**Longevity angle.** A named **5-step "Rado Health protocol"**: ① AI questionnaire → ② initial exam & Longevity plan → ③ innovative lab work & diagnostics (hormones, vitamins, minerals, heavy metals, **microbiome, genetics**) → ④ personalised treatments (hormone therapy, supplementation, nutrition, IV/NAD+) → ⑤ ongoing progress tracking. **Animation / motion observed.** A **multi-image hero carousel** ("Revolucija zdravlja / Health Revolution"); a **scrolling client-logo marquee** (Yandex, Adriatic Bank, etc. — corporate trust); treatment **sliders**; an AI-questionnaire funnel; a pop-up for a free 15-minute intro call.[5]

But put **doctor photography in the hero** — this site shows why that matters.

### 5) MAHA — Slovenia 🇸🇮
**URL:** https://maha.si · **City:** Ljubljana, Slovenska cesta 54 · **Languages:** Slovenian (language switcher present)

**What it is.** An integrative-medicine and dentistry clinic with a dedicated **"medical longevity" (medicinska dolgoživost)** service line — a more editorial, philosophical counterpoint to the tech-forward clinics above.[10][11]

**Positioning line (translated):** *"Your body doesn't give up. It adapts — to the wrong environment."* — and *"Medical longevity means recreating the conditions in which health maintains itself."*[10][11]

**Longevity angle.** Six dimensions of medical longevity: **medical diagnostics → detoxification → regeneration (mitochondria/immune: PAPIMI, IHHT, hyperthermia, ozone) → psychological integration → holistic alignment → continuous monitoring**; collaborations with labs in **Germany** and diagnostic centres in **Switzerland**.[11] It frames healthspan explicitly as *"health period + illness period."*[10]

**Team section (what to copy).** Names the practitioners rather than hiding them: **dr. Sebastjan Perko** (founder/physician), **Vaidya Ajil Kunhumbidukka Veettil** (Ayurvedic medicine), **Jana Pahole, dr. med.**, and a chef (**Erik Božič**, MAHA Bistro) — pairing medicine with culinary science.[10]

**Animation / motion observed.** Editorial large-type hero over a desaturated portrait; **stat counter ("2,500+ patients")**; illustrated "line of life" graphic (birth → healthspan → illness); bistro/food section; cookie/settings tables.[10] Motion is subtler than Epik/Notia.

**Best takeaway for a Bulgarian site.** If your brand wants to feel **human, holistic and premium** rather than clinical and technical, this is the reference: a strong founder voice, a nervous-system/integrative framing, a **verified patient-count social proof**, and **cross-disciplinary positioning** (medicine + nutrition/dentistry). Also note the **"line of life" healthspan visual** — a very shareable way to explain longevity.

## 5. "What to steal" — a practical checklist for a Bulgarian longevity clinic site

**Structure (all five converge on this):**
1. **Video or animated hero** with a claim + credibility number (Blue Longevity, Epik, Notia).
2. **3–5-step patient journey** as a numbered/scrolling visual (Epik's ring; Rado's 5 steps).
3. **Programmes/packages** with a tab or toggle and an **animated comparison** (Epik's progress bars).
4. **Named team section** with portraits, roles and specialties — the single biggest trust lever (Blue Longevity, Epik, MAHA); Notia proves the cost of omitting it.
5. **Diagnostics as proof**: biomarker counts, specific tests (TruAge, DEXA, VO₂ max, CGM, microbiome).
6. **A free lead magnet**: assessment quiz (Blue Longevity's "Longevity Score") or a downloadable guide (Epik's longevity manual).
7. **Corporate / "for business" track** for B2B revenue (Blue Longevity "For business"; Rado Corporate).
8. **Accordion FAQ** + **press/award wall** + **logo marquee** for social proof.

**Motion vocabulary to reuse:** scroll-reveal sections, letter-by-letter headline reveal, stat counters, carousels with drag, accordions with rotating +/−, progress bars, hero video loops, persona/segment sliders.

**Local design/award references in Bulgaria:** **eDesign** is the only Bulgarian agency with Awwwards/Webby/FWA/CSSDA/Lovie wins and has an in-house animation/3D department (useful if you outsource motion design)[21]; **Dr Dancheva** (dermatology) won a 2025 A' Design Award for web design — a Bulgarian medical site design precedent[22]. The **Bulgarian Longevity Association** exists as a sector body you can cite for authority[23].
