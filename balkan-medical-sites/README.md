# Regina Life Clinic Website — Asset Notes

This folder contains reference material and design assets collected while building the bilingual (Bulgarian / English) website for Regina Life Clinic, a longevity clinic based in Bulgaria.

## Contents

- `Balkans-Medical-Longevity-Sites-Report.md` — research notes and benchmark summary of comparable clinics and longevity sites in the Balkans.
- `*.png` — screenshots of the reference sites used for visual and structural benchmarking.

## Purpose

The files here informed the layout, colour palette, animation approach, and content structure for the Regina Life Clinic site. They are kept for traceability and future iteration.
